Learning paths

Start where you are. Level up with a clear roadmap.

Pick a path and follow the sequence. Each one is designed to minimize confusion, maximize repetition, and keep your practice moving forward.

Beginner

Layer-by-layer fundamentals

Go from “I can’t solve it” to your first consistent solves with a method that’s easy to remember and hard to mess up.

Suggested pace

1–2 focused sessions

  • Notation made simple (with a quick reference)
  • White cross and first-layer corners
  • Middle layer edges without confusion
  • Last layer: yellow cross, face, then corners/edges

Best for

First-time solvers and returning beginners.

Next: learn notation, build a clean white cross, then lock in the first layer with repeatable corner inserts.

Intermediate

CFOP foundations (without overload)

Build speed the right way: intuitive F2L, efficient lookahead habits, and small algorithm sets that give big returns.

Suggested pace

2–4 weeks of practice

  • Cross planning and smoother execution
  • Intuitive F2L patterns you can actually recall
  • 2-look OLL + 2-look PLL starter set
  • Timing and drill structure to break plateaus

Best for

Solvers stuck around 1–2 minutes who want real speed gains.

Next: improve cross planning, start intuitive F2L, then learn the small OLL/PLL sets that give the biggest payoff.

Advanced

Full CFOP + algorithm mastery

Turn practice into progress with a structured roadmap: full OLL/PLL, smarter F2L, and training that translates into faster solves.

Suggested pace

Ongoing progression

  • Full OLL & PLL study plan (spaced + trackable)
  • Cross optimization ideas you can apply today
  • F2L efficiency: inserts, rotations, and flow
  • Trainer-driven review so you keep what you learn

Best for

CFOP learners ready to systematize and go faster.

Next: study full OLL/PLL with spaced repetition, then refine F2L efficiency and cross execution for smoother solves.

Not sure where to start? Choose Beginner if you can’t solve the cube yet, Intermediate if you’re stuck around 1–2 minutes, and Advanced if you’re building full CFOP.
