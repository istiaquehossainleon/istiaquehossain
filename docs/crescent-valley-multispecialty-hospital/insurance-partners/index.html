How cashless support works

A guided process — from estimates to documentation.

Our billing support desk can help you understand coverage, pre-authorizations, cost estimates, and claim documentation.

  1. Step 1

    Share policy and patient details

    Provide policy information, employer authorization (if applicable), and clinical notes when available.

  2. Step 2

    Pre-authorization and estimate support

    We guide pre-authorization where applicable and share an estimate before admission whenever possible.

  3. Step 3

    Admission, billing, and coordination

    Billing desk support during admission, with guidance for approvals and payment options.

  4. Step 4

    Documentation for reimbursement

    Support for claims documentation, discharge summaries, reports, and invoices based on partner requirements.
